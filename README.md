## The entry

> This stapler has jammed once a month for five years, and HR still lists it as essential office equipment.

One line, 105 characters. The joke is the stapler's real behaviour: it jams on page two at least once a month, every month, for years — and the office still officially lists it as essential equipment. The dullness itself is the joke: the most useless reliable thing in the building, formally indispensable.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, no dependencies, no network) writes the review to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
